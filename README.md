# 终局之书 · The Book of Endings

我们比之前的任何时刻都更接近终局时刻。

我们希望《终局之书》成为一场关于人类终局的讨论：一本永远无法写完、由所有人共同编写的书。

- 每个人都可以创建自己的线路，提出自己的想法。
- 每一次投票和选择都被计数。
- 每个人都可以修改自己的选择，添加自己的选项。
- 书中的每一条线路，都会记下提出和调整它的贡献者。

---

We are closer to the moment of ending than at any time before.

We hope The Book of Endings becomes a discussion about humanity's endings: a book that can never be finished, written by everyone together.

- Anyone can create their own paths and put forward their own ideas.
- Every vote and every choice is counted.
- Everyone can change their choices and add their own options.
- Every path in the book records the contributors who proposed and adjusted it.
